What is later.me?

later.me is a tiny, self-hosted way to write letters to your future self. Write a letter, pick a date - months or years away - and seal it. When the date arrives, the letter lands in your email inbox.

Sealed means sealed

The moment you seal a letter, it's encrypted on disk. You can't read it early - not even by opening the files.

If you really must peek, it will cost you: a 24-hour cooling-off period before the letter will open, and a permanent peeked mark so future you knows. Most people who ask to peek never come back after the cooling-off. The impulse passes. The letter keeps its magic.

Yours, entirely

There's no company, no account, no server in the cloud. Everything lives on your own machine:

· Letters are stored (encrypted) in the data/ folder
· Delivery happens through your own email account over SMTP
· The encryption key is data/secret.key - back it up if your letters matter to you

later.me is open source. Clone it, run ./setup.sh, and it's yours too.

How delivery works

A small script (deliver.py) checks daily whether any sealed letter has reached its date. Due letters are decrypted and emailed to you - then never sent again. Schedule it once and forget it: one morning years from now, past you says hello.
